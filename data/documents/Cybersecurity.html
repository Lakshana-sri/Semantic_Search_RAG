Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Introduction

Cybersecurity refers to the protection of computers, networks, applications, and digital information from unauthorized access, cyberattacks, damage, and theft. It is essential for protecting personal information and organizational systems.

Common Cyber Threats

Phishing is a technique used to trick users into revealing sensitive information. Malware is malicious software designed to damage or access systems. Ransomware can encrypt files and demand payment for recovery.

Security Measures

  • Use strong and unique passwords
  • Enable multi-factor authentication
  • Install security updates
  • Use firewalls and antivirus software
  • Back up important data
  • Avoid suspicious links and attachments

Example

A student receives an email asking for their college login credentials through an unfamiliar link. Recognizing the message as a possible phishing attempt and avoiding the link helps protect the account.

Conclusion

Cybersecurity requires both technical protection and responsible user behavior to maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability of digital information.
