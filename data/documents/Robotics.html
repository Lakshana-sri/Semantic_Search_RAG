Robotics and Automation

Introduction

Robotics is a branch of engineering and computer science that focuses on designing, building, and operating robots. Automation uses technology to perform tasks with reduced human intervention.

Components of a Robot

  • Sensors for collecting information
  • Actuators for movement
  • Controllers for processing commands
  • Power supply for operation
  • Software for decision-making

Types of Robots

Industrial robots are used in manufacturing and assembly. Service robots assist humans in hospitals, homes, and public places. Mobile robots move through different environments, while autonomous robots make decisions using sensors and algorithms.

Applications

  • Manufacturing and packaging
  • Healthcare and surgery assistance
  • Warehouse management
  • Space exploration
  • Agricultural operations

Example

A warehouse robot can identify shelves, navigate through aisles, and transport products to a designated location using sensors and programmed instructions.

Conclusion

Robotics and automation improve productivity, precision, and safety in repetitive or hazardous tasks.
